Read before using

What the sample can prove

The selected records show real examples of how agencies packaged and awarded work. They cannot establish total market size, growth rate, subcontract demand, or a recompete date.

Official sources only

USAspending award search and detail records, human-readable USAspending award pages, and SAM.gov notices.

Curated, not exhaustive

The initial pull screened 2,114 unique records. Forty-five were selected for clearly described, recurring patterns.

Amounts stay separate

Obligations, exercised options, and potential values are different fields. Vehicle potential values are not treated as spend.

No forecast claims

An end date does not prove a recompete, and a prime award does not prove the company is seeking subcontractors.

The honest boundary

A radar is context, not a pipeline.

This public sample is for understanding past and current buying patterns. Any live pursuit still requires the current notice, attachments, vehicle access, conflict review, and a buyer-specific go or no-go decision.
